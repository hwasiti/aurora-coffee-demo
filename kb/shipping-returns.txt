AURORA COFFEE ROASTERS - SHIPPING & RETURNS POLICY
Keileweg 18, 3029 BS Rotterdam, Netherlands
Last updated: August 2026

=== SHIPPING ===

ROAST & DISPATCH SCHEDULE
We roast every Tuesday. Every order is dispatched within 48 hours of
roasting. Orders placed after Monday 23:59 CET are roasted the
following Tuesday. You will receive a tracking link by email the
moment your parcel leaves the roastery.

RATES
- Orders over EUR 35: FREE shipping (EU-wide)
- Orders under EUR 35: flat EUR 4.95
- We ship to EU countries only. UK and Switzerland are planned.

DELIVERY TIMES (after dispatch)
- Netherlands & Belgium: 1-2 business days
- Germany & France: 2-3 business days
- Rest of EU: 2-4 business days

CARRIER
All parcels ship with PostNL or DPD. Parcels fit through most
letterboxes (our 250 g bags are letterbox-friendly up to 2 bags).

ADDRESS CHANGES
Need to change the delivery address? Email us within 2 hours of
placing the order and we can still update it. After that the order
enters our fulfilment queue and cannot be modified.

=== RETURNS & ISSUES ===

UNOPENED BAGS
Return unopened bags within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Email hello@auroracoffee.example with your order number; we will send
a prepaid return label. Refunds are issued within 5 business days of
the bags arriving back at the roastery.

OPENED BAGS - BREW GUARANTEE
If you opened a bag and genuinely do not enjoy the coffee, the Brew
Guarantee applies: we send a free replacement bag of a different
coffee, no return needed. Claimable once per order, within 30 days.

DAMAGED OR LOST PARCELS
If your order arrives damaged, send a photo of the parcel and its
contents to hello@auroracoffee.example within 7 days of delivery and
we will re-ship your full order free of charge - no questions asked.
If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, we treat the parcel as lost
and immediately send a replacement.

WRONG ITEM RECEIVED
Keep the wrong item (consider it a gift) and we will ship the correct
one within one business day of your email.

Questions? Ask the chat widget on auroracoffee or email
hello@auroracoffee.example - we answer within one business day.
