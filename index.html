Our Story

Two friends, one tiny roaster, zero compromises.

Aurora was founded in 2021 in Rotterdam by Mara and Tomás, who met arguing over a burnt batch of Kenyan beans at a roasting course — and decided they could do better together.

Today we roast in small 15-kilo batches on a restored 1987 Probat, source directly from twelve farms we visit ourselves, and pay on average 38% above Fairtrade minimums.

Quality control is handled by Peaberry, our espresso-brown dachshund and Chief Nap Officer, who has never once approved a bad batch.

Quick answers

Frequently asked

When will my order ship?

We roast every Tuesday and ship every order within 48 hours of roasting. Most EU orders arrive within 2–4 business days of dispatch.

Is shipping free?

Yes — free shipping across the EU on orders over €35. Below that, a flat €4.95 applies.

Can I return coffee?

Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days. Opened a bag and not happy? Our Brew Guarantee has you covered — see the full FAQ.
