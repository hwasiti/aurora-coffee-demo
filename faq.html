Frequently Asked Questions

Aurora Coffee Roasters — Rotterdam. Last updated August 2026.

1. When do you roast and ship?

We roast every Tuesday in small 15-kilo batches, and every order ships within 48 hours of roasting. Orders placed after Monday 23:59 CET go into the following week's roast. Most EU deliveries arrive within 2–4 business days of dispatch.

2. How much is shipping?

Shipping is free across the EU on all orders over €35. For orders under €35 we charge a flat rate of €4.95. We currently ship to EU countries only; UK and Switzerland are on our roadmap.

3. What is your return policy?

Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund — just email hello@auroracoffee.example with your order number.

4. What is the Brew Guarantee?

If you open a bag and genuinely don't enjoy it, our Brew Guarantee applies: we will send you a replacement bag of a different coffee, free of charge, no return needed. The Brew Guarantee can be used once per order and must be claimed within 30 days of delivery.

5. Is your decaf really chemical-free?

Yes. Our Solstice Decaf is processed with the Swiss Water® Process, which uses only water, temperature, and time — no chemical solvents. The result is 99.9% caffeine-free.

6. How does the subscription work?

Subscribers save 10% on every delivery and choose a delivery rhythm of every 1, 2, or 4 weeks. You can pause, skip a delivery, or cancel at any time from your account page — there are no cancellation fees.

7. What is the Aurora Beans loyalty programme?

Every euro you spend earns 1 Aurora Bean. Once you have collected 100 Beans, you automatically get €5 off your next order. Beans never expire, and subscribers earn them too.

8. Which grind options do you offer?

Every coffee is available as whole bean, espresso grind, filter grind, or French-press grind. If you are unsure, we recommend whole bean — freshness lasts far longer, and any entry-level burr grinder will outperform pre-ground within a week.

9. How should I store my coffee?

Keep the bag sealed in a cool, dark cupboard — never in the fridge. Our bags have a one-way valve and resealable zip; used properly, the coffee stays at peak flavour for about 6 weeks after the roast date printed on the bag.

10. Do you sell wholesale?

Yes — we supply cafés, offices, and restaurants across the Benelux. Wholesale pricing starts at a minimum order of 5 kg per month. Contact wholesale@auroracoffee.example with your volumes and we will reply within two business days.

11. Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept iDEAL, all major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and Bancontact. Subscriptions require a card or PayPal.

12. Who is the dog in your photos?

That's Peaberry, our espresso-brown dachshund and Chief Nap Officer. He has been with us since the first roast in 2021 and takes his quality-control duties (napping next to the roaster) extremely seriously.

13. Can I visit the roastery?

Roastery visits and public cuppings happen on the first Saturday of every month at Keileweg 18, Rotterdam. Book a spot by emailing hello@auroracoffee.example — it's free, and yes, Peaberry attends.
